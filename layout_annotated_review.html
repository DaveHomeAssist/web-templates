How to use this page

A plain-language explanation of what this review is and how to read it. Tell each audience where to look.

  • Primary reader: the red and orange items at the top are the ones that need action first; the green section near the bottom is what already looks fine.
  • Specialist / second reviewer: blue Legal note / expert boxes flag the items where their read matters most. Everything is sourced to the original text.
  • Use the filter buttons to show only one priority, or print the page (it is formatted to print cleanly).

One caveat up front: state the scope and any disclaimer here (e.g. "practical review, not legal advice").
